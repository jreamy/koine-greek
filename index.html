        Χαίρετε!

        This page is a collection of resources I have put together for practicing Koine Greek. Currently it's primarily geared toward using Croy (A Primer of Biblical Greek), but as time goes on I may add other resources.

        Flashcards

        In my opinion, making your own flashcards is better than using someone else's, but I thought these might help get y'all started. You'll notice a few types of cards:

        • vocab word to definition
        • sentence fragment (very important!)
        • verb or noun paradigm
        • a grammatical rule or pattern

        Set a modest goal of how much you will practice and then stick with it! I generally aim for 5 - 10 new cards each day, then practice every day for 10 - 15 minutes.

        Reading Alone

        I've found it very effective to:

        1. Read the text out loud
        2. Copy the text by hand with space underneath
          (until you're really picking up speed with writing)
        3. Translate the text word-by-word underneath the Greek
        4. Write a more natural-sounding English translation
        5. Read the text out loud again thinking about your word-by-word translation
        6. Make any flashcards of sentence fragments that seem memorable

        Reading With Others

        One of the best ways I've found to study Greek is reading aloud with others – I encourage you to find friends to read with. The example sentences from Croy are a decent starting point and jumping into the New Testament will benefit you in untold ways. Don't worry if you don't understand a single word of the Gospel at first; read it in Greek, read it in English, then read it in Greek again and you'll start to subconsciously pick up parts of the language. If the Croy sentences are too easy, but jumping into the New Testament is too daunting, check out some of the readers I've put together.
